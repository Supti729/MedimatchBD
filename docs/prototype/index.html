How it works

Six steps from sign-up to a completed handover.

  1. 1

    Register

    Sign up with a phone number or email and choose your area.

  2. 2

    Search or list

    Look for a medicine, post a request, or list one you can give.

  3. 3

    Get matched

    Requests are compared with available listings near you.

  4. 4

    Verify when required

    Some medicines need a prescription check by an authorized reviewer.

  5. 5

    Connect

    Chat privately inside the platform. No phone numbers shared.

  6. 6

    Complete

    Arrange a safe handover, then mark the exchange complete.

Built around privacy and safety

These are the rules the platform is designed around. Final policies will follow applicable Bangladesh regulations.

  • Private profiles

    Others see only your name, photo and a trust badge. Never your phone, email or address.

  • In-platform chat

    Talk to the other person without sharing contact details.

  • Verification workflow

    Prescriptions go to an authorized reviewer, not to the donor. The donor sees only the result.

  • Reporting

    Report a listing or user. Admins review and record every decision.

  • Controlled listings

    Expired, damaged or unidentified medicine cannot be listed.

  • Not medical advice

    This platform does not diagnose or recommend treatment. Ask a doctor or pharmacist before taking any medicine.
